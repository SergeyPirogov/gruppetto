        Strava

        Finished rides upload to Strava as a Virtual Ride, with power, heart rate and cadence. Gruppetto only requests upload access and reads none of your data.

        Garmin Connect

        Finished rides go to Garmin Connect as a FIT file. Garmin has no upload API for apps like this one, so Gruppetto signs in the way Garmin's own app does. Your email and password pass once through Gruppetto's server on to Garmin and are kept nowhere. What comes back is a sign-in that stays only in this browser, and it opens your whole Garmin account, so connect only on your own device. Rather not? Save the .FIT after the ride and upload it at connect.garmin.com yourself.
